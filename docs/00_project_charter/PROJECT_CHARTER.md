### Summary

Rent-a-Cow is a digital platform to reconnect people with responsible livestock stewardship through transparency, technology, and lifelong relationships. We enable your righteous path to serve atleast one cow in a lifetime while ensuring transparency and trust in your vedic endeavour.

### Vision

To eliminate the geographical and infrastructural limitations of urban life we provide professionally  managed cows and enable any individual worldwide to establish lifelong relationship through continous, transparent serving.

### Mission

To enable transparent, ethical and professionally managed lifelong cow ownership accessible from anywhere in the world.

* Religious Inspiration

     The platform is inspired by the Hindu traditions of Gau-Seva and Gau-Daan.

### Problem Statement

* Urban disconnection: Modern urban and overseas living prevents individuals from physical cow ownership.
* Transparency deficit: Existing gaushala management systems operates without accountability of the cows' feeding, caring and medical well-being.

* Personal Relationship : While the existing gaushalas accept charity but they do not establish a cow to an individual, we forge companionship of a lifetime.
* Fragmented Dairy Chains : Spurious dairy products continue to promise consumers. We give back all the dairy products of your owned cow with full accountablity of her diet and other vitals.

* Vedic Rituals: Santana Dharma dictates to own atleast a cow and certain rituals require "gau-daan" which is presently performed by either modelling a silver/gold made murti as neither the giver or the taker has the provision to serve the cow.

### Why this Product Exists

The product is born out of a profound Puranic injuction : *the spirtiual obligation of a hindu to serve and care for a cow.*

This product does not exist as a mere commercial diary enterprise or a passive charity app. It exists as a digital sanctuary to execute a core life milestone with absolute mental, operational and offering(by-products- dailry) certainity.

### Product Philosophy

* Absolute Livestock Supremacy: This is not a milk producing farm where cows are reared unscroupsouly to bear preganancy to yield milk. The owner requests for bearing calf are reviewed and approved strictly based on animal welfare.
* Show, Do and hear me!: We understand the bond between human and animal must be transparent so all our actions towards the cow are accountable not merely via audits but uncompromisingly while the actions are being performed - not limited to feeding, caring, medical supervising.
* Complete Stewardship: All the milk products and dung-cakes are logistically dispatched to the owners. The offsprings can be sold to the farm at stipulated price.

### Target Audience

* Conscious Devotee: NRI, Tier-1 city residents who have realised the karmic obligations of life.

* Wellness Purists: Health and mind conscious individuals who want uncompromised, tracebaly pure, ethically extracted dairy products extracted from owner defined and farm approved nutritous diet.
* The Next-Gen Lineage: Urban parents who want to descend the karm-kands to their children through immersive farm telemetry and physical visits.

### Core Value Proposition

* Flawless religious karmic accomplishment
* Absolute Operational Certainity
* Ethical Cow Husbandry
* Full ownership of Cows' produce

### Guiding Principles

* Embrace Kindness: All actions in the farm are determined by kindness towards the animal. The software manifests this transmission of energy in the form of live audio/video stream.
* Immutable Integrity: All actions at farm are auditable at the time of event and post the event.
* Two way Communication: We hear your opinions at the time of actions and proceed accordingly considering the best outcome for the animal. We take your requests on cows' diet, meal times, offspring bearing, frequency of milking, time spent under the open sky, time spent under the shed and tethered, cleanliness, medical recoomendation.
* Lifetime Bonding: Our goal is to develop a bond between the cow and the owner, by involving the owner in all possible actions,giving back all the produces of the cow, planning a physical visit to the farm wherever possible.
